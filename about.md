I am **Arata Hoshino**, a strategic and venture designer based in the United Arab Emirates. I was
raised in Japan, and spent my teens reading about Silicon Valley technology, longtermism,
techno-libertarianism, and the ideas of Buckminster Fuller.

I hold a BEng in Mechanical and Energy Engineering from Waseda University, an MSc in Media Design
from Keio University, where my research was in service design and venture design, and a CEMS MIM
from HEC Paris and the National University of Singapore. After that I worked in venture capital
and in strategy consulting, in San Francisco and in Singapore. From 2024 to 2026 I advised UAE
ministries on economic strategy at a policy advisory firm spun out of the OECD and Harvard.

Much of the skeleton of this thesis is owed to
[Kei Irizawa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kei-irizawa-211800121/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
(Yale University), a classmate since junior high school, through continuing conversation and our
joint memoranda, "Energy Supply and Computational Capacity for the Post-Capitalist Society" and
"Roadmap of Next 10 Years". He also reviewed the data and the structure of the argument. The
errors that remain are mine.
