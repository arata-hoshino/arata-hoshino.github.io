Four years across strategy consulting, venture, and sovereign product work in Singapore, San
Francisco, Tokyo, and Dubai. At Whiteshield in Dubai I orchestrate cross-functional delivery of
AI products and policy platforms with UAE federal ministries and a national bank.

These essays are the other half of that work. Sovereign platforms fail for the same reasons
small services do — coordination costs, half-finished migrations, budgets nobody wrote down —
and writing is how I keep those lessons portable.

## Vision

I have always wanted to change the world through technology. I am convinced that by achieving
energy abundance and the radical automation of production and transportation, we can liberate
humanity from drudgery and fundamentally transform people's lifestyles, economic structures,
and social contracts.

As its intellectual foundation, I am co-authoring working papers on removing energy supply
constraints and designing a physically automated society. Their central argument: macro
indicators such as stagnant growth and declining birth rates are **symptoms, not causes**; just
as easing the food constraint once liberated population growth, they will resolve endogenously
once physical supply constraints are removed. My goal is to realise this transition at the
frontier — through venture investment and venture development.

## Selected work

1. **UAE Skills · UAE MoHRE × UAE MoHESR.** A national AI platform connecting education,
   skills, and the labour market. Product owner for the citizen-facing interfaces: personas,
   journeys, user stories, design direction, sprints to QA. National launch, 20,000 users,
   1.2M students in scope.
2. **Branch & ATM Network Platform · the UAE's largest bank.** AI siting recommendations the
   C-suite decides relocation and site selection on. Service designer and product owner:
   discovery workshops, IA, personas and journeys, wireframes, geospatial ground truth. Live
   nationwide.
3. **SME Procurement Platform · Abu Dhabi government entity.** A platform routing corporate and
   government spend to local SMEs. Service designer: contextual inquiry, personas and journeys,
   lo-fi wireframes, 114 business and 355 functional requirements.
4. **Service Design for Consultants · PwC Cybersecurity × CSIRT.** Making explicit how a
   consultant reads a client's values and internal politics. Solo researcher and designer:
   ethnography under NDA, a handbook system, a two-cycle proof of concept.
5. **Service Design at Tokyo International Airport.** Turning waiting time into exploration by
   moving the calculation onto the luggage cart. Researcher and service designer: a month of
   participant observation, 100+ passengers observed, service blueprint, UI flows.

## This site

Every essay is a Markdown file in `_essays/`. Jekyll reads the front matter, sorts by the
`order` field, and builds the table of contents, the index on the front page, and the pager at
the foot of each essay. There is no database and no editor — a new essay is a new file, a
commit, and a push. The type is set in Jost, after Futura, in the system used by the portfolio
deck above.
